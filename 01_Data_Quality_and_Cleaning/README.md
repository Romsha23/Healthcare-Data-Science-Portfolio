# Phase 1: Data Quality & Cleaning

This phase focuses on the foundation of healthcare analytics: ensuring data is reliable and privacy-compliant. 

**Key Objectives:**
* Identify missing or duplicate patient records.
* Validate clinical measurements (e.g., filtering out biologically impossible heart rates).
* Validate temporal consistency (e.g., ensuring discharge dates do not precede admission dates).
* Apply HIPAA Safe Harbor rules to de-identify Protected Health Information (PHI).
